# Query lifetime research

Implementation studies and benchmark methodology are maintained in the
[repository's internal research notes](https://github.com/gweiermann/your-language/blob/main/.agents/implementation/research/query-lifetimes.md).

Language authors can follow the [query model](/guide/queries) and the
[core query reference](/api/declarative-queries). Cache retention and scheduling
are engine details, not controls a language definition must configure.
